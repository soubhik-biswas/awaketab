---
title: The extension, mini clock, widgets and wall mode in the Glance design
date: 2026-10-31
type: changed
---

- The Chrome extension's popup shows your clock face, four lengths and one button: keep this tab awake, pause, resume or stop. It works in any tab and while you use other apps.
- The toolbar icon counts down the minutes on green, shows II in amber when paused and ! in red when something blocks it.
- Everything in the extension is free, including keeping chosen sites awake automatically. Add a site straight from the page you are on.
- A new option releases the screen while the tab is hidden and picks up again when you come back, and a gentle chime can play when the time is up.
- The extension no longer asks to show notifications, and asks for a site only when you add it.
- The floating mini clock is smaller, shows your face and closes by itself when the session ends.
- Home-screen widgets come in large, small and a one-tap quick start.
- Wall mode puts the face full screen on pure black, shifts it every few minutes to protect the screen and dims to 40% after 10 PM.
