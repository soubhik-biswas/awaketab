date: 2026-11-14
type: changed

- All six vibes are now drawn live on your graphics chip: northern lights that fold over a mirror lake, a sky that slowly turns above a hillside town, a city through a wet window with drops that bend the lights, the sun setting into a glittering sea, glossy wax rising in a lava lamp, and window light with swaying leaf shadows. Each has its own daylight version.
- They stay light on battery: drawn at 24 or 30 frames a second below full resolution, paused in hidden tabs, and still for Still, reduced motion and the previews. Browsers without WebGL keep the previous scenes.
- Every clock face got real materials and light: sunray and enamel dials with raised markers and steel hands, glass tubes and lit segments, paper, porcelain and frosted glass by day, and finer ticks and type throughout.
- Faces that showed no state word now name it, and the time left is never covered by a hand.
