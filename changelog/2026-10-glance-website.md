---
title: The website in the Glance design
date: 2026-10-31
type: changed
---

- Below the clock, the home page now shows a moving gallery of faces, six living backgrounds, what makes AwakeTab different, three illustrated steps, use cases, straight answers and one big button to start.
- Every page has a floating glass menu, follows your system's light or dark setting, and ends on a new footer with a feedback card, a live status pill and a theme switch.
- The question about counting visits is a small card in the corner instead of a block in the page, and both answers look the same.
- Questions open and close properly, one at a time, with a plus or minus that shows which.
- There is no support email any more: ask from Settings → Send feedback inside the timer.
- Each page has its own share image, and the changelog has an RSS feed.
