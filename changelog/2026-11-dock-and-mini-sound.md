---
title: A lighter dock and focus sound in the mini clock
date: 2026-11-13
type: changed
---

- On computers and tablets the dock is smaller and calmer, and it no longer touches the scroll hint under it.
- The mini clock has its own focus sound row: see what plays, start or pause it, and step through the scenes without going back to the tab.
- In Chrome and Edge the mini clock drops its own back-to-tab and size buttons, since the browser's window already has them; drag the window to resize it.
- The light that runs around the Keep awake button now follows the button's rounded edge exactly, at an even speed.
