- All six vibes are new scenes: northern lights over a mountain lake, a turning night sky over a small town, a city through rain on glass, the sun setting into the sea, glossy lava wax, and calm window light. Each has its own daylight version.
- Still, Calm and Lively now really differ, and the area behind the clock stays quiet so every face reads clearly.
- The mini clock comes in two sizes, compact and expanded, with pause, +15 min, stop, back to tab and focus sound.
- In Chrome and Edge on a computer, leaving the tab during a session can open the mini clock by itself. Your browser decides and may ask once; where it will not, AwakeTab offers it with one tap. You can turn this off in Settings.
- Focus sound keeps playing while you are on another tab during a session, and the dock has a button for the mini clock.
