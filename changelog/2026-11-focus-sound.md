- Seventeen sounds in four groups: rain, ocean, stream, wind, crickets and thunder; café, fireplace, train and fan; brown, pink and white noise; and four kinds of music made live in your browser.
- The music never loops: a warm pad, soft keys, a low drone and glass bells drift slowly through the same key, so they always sound right together.
- Eight one-tap scenes, from Deep focus to Summer night, and a Shuffle that always makes a pleasant mix.
- One master volume, with a limiter so stacked sounds never distort, and a gentle fade when a session starts, pauses or ends.
- Nothing is downloaded: every sound is made on your device.
