title: 46 clock faces, each with a real light version
date: 2026-10-31
type: changed
---

- Every face now has a true light version: porcelain flip cards, frosted Nixie tubes, a white departure board, a white-washed neon wall, a clear Liquid sphere, a pale HUD, a dawn Constellation sky, a bright Eclipse sky, a daytime Moon sky and a pastel Aurora. E-ink turns into night paper in dark and OLED.
- Dark dials move to a graphite material with a soft glow of the light colour at the bottom edge; OLED dials are pure black with a thin outline.
- LED, Pulse and Terminal are retired. If you used one, AwakeTab now shows LCD (for LED and Terminal) or Waveform (for Pulse).
- The gallery groups the faces as Featured 18, Digital, Analog, Ambient and All 46, and Ring is the default face.
- Clock of clocks reads one hour as "1:00", and Diver's time left sits in a raised pill that the hands never cover.
