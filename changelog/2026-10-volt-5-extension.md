- The popup, welcome page and settings use the new colours and Archivo headings: violet Keep awake, a volt-green lamp while the screen is held, and a calm grey when time is up.
- The toolbar icon lights its lamp only while AwakeTab is keeping the device awake, and shows it hollow otherwise; the minutes-left badge is unchanged.
- The popup's main button reads "Keep awake · 45 min", as on the website, and the time's up notification shows the mark with its ended lamp.
