---
title: Every clock face, redrawn with care
date: 2026-11-13
type: fixed
---

- Flip is a real split-flap: when a number changes, its top half folds over the hinge, and only the card that changed moves.
- Rolling digits roll at the moment they change, and Tape shows its ruler at any length, including no end.
- Nothing is cut at the edge of a face any more: glows, hands, labels and readouts stay inside, at one hour, ten hours and no end.
- Eclipse, Hourglass, Battery and Gauge show the time left accurately, and Radar sweeps the right way.
- Night faces keep their digits readable over bright colour, and every face names its state truthfully: ready, awake, paused, needs a tap or time's up.
- Labels stay readable on small screens and short windows, and Hindi, Japanese and Chinese labels are no longer spaced like capitals.
- The glow around a moving dot stays on the dot, and the second ring around the clock is gone.
