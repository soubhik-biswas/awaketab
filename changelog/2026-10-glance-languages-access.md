---
title: The timer in eight languages, and easier to use by keyboard
date: 2026-10-31
type: changed
---

- The timer, its panels and the home page now read in German, Japanese, Chinese, Hindi, Spanish, French and Brazilian Portuguese, with the time written the way each language writes it.
- Keyboard focus goes where you expect: the status, the length, Keep awake, then Vibe, Faces, Recents, Settings and Search. "Skip to the timer" takes you straight there.
- One-key shortcuts work only while the timer has focus, so the space bar scrolls the page below it as usual.
- Every focus ring sits a little away from its button, sliders are easier to grab, and the timer reflows on a 320 px screen or at 200% zoom.
- New favicons and app icons with the progress-ring logo.
